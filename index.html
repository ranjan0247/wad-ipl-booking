Stadium Guidelines & Instructions

🎟️ Ticket Verification

E-tickets or physical printed tickets must be presented at the entry gates along with a valid barcode scan. Re-entry is strictly prohibited once inside the stadium premises.

🪪 ID Proof Requirement

All attendees must carry a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driver's License, Passport, or PAN Card) matching the name on the ticket for age and security verification.

🚫 Prohibited Items

Outside food, beverages, laptops, helmets, power banks, lighters, cameras with detachable lenses, and any sharp or inflammable items are strictly banned inside the stadium.
